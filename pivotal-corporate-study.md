# UL CV for Pivotal Corporate -text only

## Pivotal Corporate Teamwork

While working at O’Mahony’s, an order of books was sent to the wrong library because the invoice I received contained an incorrect delivery address.

I raised the issue quickly with my supervisor and proposed sending the receiving library a pre-paid delivery label so the books could be forwarded directly to the correct library.

I helped coordinate communication with both client libraries so the receiving library understood what had happened and the intended library received its order with minimal delay and no additional cost. The experience taught me the importance of teamwork, high standards of client care, responsiveness, accurate information, practical problem solving and reliable follow-through.

At UL, Computer Software 1 also gave me experience of collaborative learning through a Java pair-programming project, where we divided tasks, reviewed work together and solved problems collaboratively. I am comfortable contributing in team meetings, supporting colleagues and working across different functions in a fast-paced environment.



## Pivotal Corporate Communication Skills

My retail, logistics and university experience has developed strong written and verbal communication skills. At O’Mahony’s, clear communication is important when working with orders, invoices, dispatch information, different departments and client libraries. At Mr Price, I dealt directly with customer product and pricing queries.

My university and project work have also taught me to communicate financial and technical information at the appropriate level. For example, I used Excel to compare three PCP finance options and presented the overall cost clearly rather than simply presenting the calculations.

I understand that at Pivotal the same approach is required when developing business-like relationships with clients, intermediaries and internal clients: listen carefully, communicate accurately, be accessible and responsive, and provide a high-quality professional service with reliable follow-through.



## Pivotal Corporate Problem Solving and Analytics

My Financial Mathematics degree has developed my critical-thinking and analytical skills through Financial Accounting, Accounting for Financial Decision Making, Finance, Applied Data Analysis, Numerical Analysis, Operations Research, Fundamentals of Financial Mathematics, Statistical Inference and Advanced Data Analysis.

I enjoy identifying differences, checking assumptions and working systematically towards a solution. In my Leaving Certificate Applied Mathematics project, I independently researched air resistance beyond the syllabus and incorporated it into my model.

I have also practised account-reconciliation thinking in Excel, including IF, nested IF, IFS, AND, OR, IFERROR, XLOOKUP, SUMIFS and COUNTIFS. I see direct links between those skills and journal entries, account reconciliations, accounts payable and accounts receivable, accurate financial records, supporting documentation and financial reporting.

For larger datasets, I understand when another tool is more appropriate. In my aircraft project I used Python and Pandas to combine OpenSky and PlaneSpotters data and work from more than 500,000 records down to the relevant 61 aircraft.



## Pivotal Corporate Using Initiative

I try to be proactive and self-motivated and to identify what needs to be done rather than dealing only with the immediate problem.

At Mr Price, when an item had no clear pricing information, I checked the correct price for the customer and also corrected the missing shelf information so the same problem would be less likely to recur. I also became actively involved during a major store-floor reorganisation.

My independent project work reflects the same positive attitude, enthusiasm and desire to take on responsibility. I regularly work beyond formal course requirements when I think it will improve my understanding or the result.

I would bring that approach to special projects, process-improvement initiatives and operational improvement at Pivotal: understand the task, contribute ideas appropriately, work within company policies and accounting standards, and follow the work through.



## Pivotal Corporate Projects, Portfolio and Volunteering

I maintain a personal project portfolio and website at [erikdownes.github.io](http://erikdownes.github.io) where I apply university learning to practical finance, accounting, data-analysis and programming problems.

My tools include Excel, Word, Outlook, Python, Pandas, R, SQL, Git and GitHub. Computer Software 1 developed my Java programming and collaborative coding skills, while maintaining the website has also given me practical experience with HTML, CSS and JavaScript.

Projects include aircraft data reconciliation and reporting, financial calculators, Excel business analysis and work combining Strava GPX data with public data, where I had to standardise datetime formats and time zones before merging records.

I have practised Excel formulas, logical functions, lookups, data cleaning, filtering, pivot tables and charts. These projects have taught me that the important skill is not simply knowing software, but choosing the right tool for the size and purpose of the problem.

I am particularly interested in using technology to support research into financial, accounting and market topics, improve processes and make information clearer for colleagues and clients.



## Pivotal Corporate Additional Information

I am a third-year Financial Mathematics student at the University of Limerick with a current QCA of 3.40 and strong results across finance, accounting, mathematics, statistics and computing. I achieved A2 grades in Finance and Accounting for Financial Decision Making, H1 Business in my Leaving Certificate and received the Kolvenbach Medal for Business at Crescent College.

Pivotal particularly interests me because it is an independent, solutions-driven firm helping US-based investors establish and grow their European investments. I am interested in how Accounting & Administration services support a portfolio of clients across different jurisdictions and allow clients to concentrate on their core business needs.

I understand that the Co-op role supports senior staff and the Client Service Teams with company administration, journal entries and account reconciliations, accounts payable and accounts receivable, VAT, tax and regulatory reporting, accurate financial records and supporting documentation, research, internal schedules and registers, KYC and CDD, statutory requirements, and regulatory and legal documentation.

I am also interested in the responsible use of AI in professional work. I have used ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini and have followed the development of large language models and more agentic systems. I understand the importance of human review, company policy, confidentiality, data protection and appropriate enterprise tools when working with client information.

I would bring an organised, detail-oriented, positive and collaborative approach, the ability to manage multiple tasks, a genuine interest in financial services and a genuine desire to learn how high-quality accounting and administration can add value to a client’s business.

## Job Description  Original

### Pivotal Corporate – Co-Op Student/Corporate Administrator

Pivotal Corporate is an independent, solutions driven firm with extensive experience assisting US based investors to establish and grow their European investments. We partner with our clients at every stage in the process using our local networks in a number of jurisdictions to ensure their project runs smoothly from the initial assessment phase through to the project being fully operational.

With our client focused and solutions driven delivery, we have the ability to adapt to the ever-changing needs of our clients quickly and efficiently, becoming the pivot point for their business and allowing them to concentrate on their core business needs as they grow.

Delivery is what sets us apart from other firms. Our clients appreciate our unrivalled responsiveness and our passion and dedication. We pride ourselves on being accessible, reliable, practical, and professional at all times.

We are currently seeking to hire a pro-active, self-motivated, and highly organised student to join the business. This role will support our Client Service Teams in providing Accounting & Administration services to a portfolio of clients, ensuring that our clients are provided with a high-quality professional service.

### Duties and Responsibilities:

- To develop a basic understanding of the mechanics of company administration and develop business like relationships with intermediaries and internal clients with whom you will come into regular contact.
- Demonstrate a willingness to apply high standards of client care to all dealings with intermediaries and internal clients.
- Duties as set out by senior members of staff including (but not limited to):
- Support the processing of journal entries and account reconciliations.
- Assist with accounts payable and accounts receivable activities
- Assist with VAT, tax and regulatory reporting processes
- Help maintain accurate financial records and supporting documentation
- Support compliance with company policies and accounting standards
- Support special projects and process improvement initiatives.
- Conduct research into financial, accounting and market topics as required.
- Participate in team meetings and contribute ideas for operational improvement
- Deal with day-to-day administration tasks assigned by company secretarial group including (but not limited to):
- Maintenance of internal schedules and registers;
- Process KYC and CDD requirement to ensure compliance with statutory requirements; and
- Organise and facilitate for the signing of regulatory and legal documentation.

### Job Specific Requirements:

- Organised, detail-oriented individual with well-developed critical thinking skills and possesses the ability to manage multiple tasks in a fast-paced environment.
- Strong written and verbal skills.
- Working knowledge of Microsoft Word, Excel, and Outlook.
- A positive attitude, demonstrating enthusiasm and the desire to take on responsibility. A willingness to get involved in team issues and events.
- An interest in financial services, and a genuine desire to learn how this can be used as a tool for adding value to a client’s business.
