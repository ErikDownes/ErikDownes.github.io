# Pivotal Corporate Exposition

This is the **working study page** for Pivotal Corporate. Read about the company, role, accounting, administration and corporate-services notes here and  enjoy learning and knowing whatever sticks. The other **Pivotal Corporate Interview** QAs is what counts for interview.

## Company

Pivotal Corporate is an **independent, solutions-driven corporate services firm** that helps **US-based investors establish and grow their businesses in Europe**.

It supports clients from the **initial assessment stage through to becoming fully operational**, using local knowledge and networks across different jurisdictions.

## Client Services

The role supports Pivotal's **Client Service Teams** in providing **accounting and administration services** to a portfolio of clients.

The important idea is that the intern's work is part of a wider client deliverable. Accuracy, responsiveness, documentation and follow-through all matter because other people rely on the work.

## Company Secretarial

**Company Secretarial is an internal function within Pivotal.**

It is **not** the secretary of the US client company. It is a professional corporate-governance and compliance function supporting client companies with areas such as statutory records, company filings, board and shareholder paperwork, resolutions, registers and other company-law requirements.

## Who can give you tasks?

Tasks may come from **senior members of the Client Services team**, including accountants, company administrators and company secretarial staff, depending on the work being handled for the client.

Work may be **assigned directly**, created as an **action point**, come through a **shared inbox or workflow**, or sit in a **task queue** for an appropriate team member to pick up.

## Initiative

Initiative does **not** mean acting outside your authority.

It means noticing appropriate work that needs to be done, picking up tasks where the process allows it, keeping the workflow moving, following through, and asking or escalating when something needs a more senior decision.

## Accounting & Administration

This area should hold the detailed notes on **journal entries, reconciliations, AP/AR, financial records, supporting documentation, company administration, KYC/CDD, registers, deadlines and client-service processes**.

## Working Relationships

Think about three groups:

- **Clients** — the external companies receiving the service.
- **Internal clients** — colleagues and teams who depend on your work.
- **Intermediaries** — external professional parties involved in getting work completed.

The common standard across all three is **professional communication, reliability, accuracy and follow-through**.
