## Teamwork

While working at **O’Mahony’s**, an order of books was sent to the wrong library because the invoice I received contained an incorrect delivery address. I raised the issue quickly with my supervisor and proposed sending the receiving library a **pre-paid delivery label** so the books could be forwarded directly to the correct library.

I helped coordinate communication with both **client libraries** so the receiving library understood what had happened and the intended library received its order with **minimal delay and no additional cost**. The experience taught me the importance of **teamwork, accurate information, practical problem solving and reliable follow-through**.

At UL, **Computer Software 1** also gave me experience of collaborative work through a Java pair-programming project, where we divided tasks, reviewed work together and solved problems jointly.

For Cashbook, I would bring the same approach to the **TIMS implementation team**: take direction, share information clearly, support colleagues and work constructively with customers and technical staff when implementing or troubleshooting a finance process.

## Communication Skills

My retail, logistics and university experience has developed **strong written and verbal communication skills**. At O’Mahony’s, clear communication is important when working with **orders, invoices, dispatch information, different departments and client libraries**. At Mr Price, I dealt directly with customer product and pricing queries.

My university and project work have also taught me that quantitative communication is not finished when the calculation is correct. The numbers still have to be made understandable to the person making the decision.

A good example was when **my mother was choosing between three car-finance offers**. I put the offers into **Excel**, calculated the total cost of each option and presented the comparison visually. The key conclusion was that the middle option was about **€800 cheaper overall**, which made the decision much clearer.

That is directly relevant to a role described as a **junior business consultant**. At Cashbook, I would need to listen carefully to customers, understand the finance process they are trying to improve, explain technical or financial information clearly, and communicate problems accurately when liaising with the implementation and IT teams.

## Problem Solving and Analytics

My Financial Mathematics degree has developed **critical-thinking and analytical skills** through Financial Accounting, Accounting for Financial Decision Making, Finance, Applied Data Analysis, Numerical Analysis, Operations Research, Financial Mathematics, Statistical Inference and Advanced Data Analysis.

I enjoy **identifying differences, checking assumptions and working systematically towards a solution**. In my Leaving Certificate Applied Mathematics project, I independently researched air resistance beyond the syllabus and incorporated it into my model.

I have practised **reconciliation thinking in Excel**, using IF, nested IF, IFS, AND, OR, IFERROR, XLOOKUP, SUMIFS and COUNTIFS to compare records, classify differences and flag exceptions.

For larger datasets, I understand when another tool is more appropriate. In my aircraft project I used **Python and Pandas** to combine and reconcile data from more than **500,000 records** down to the relevant aircraft.

Those skills connect strongly with Cashbook’s work in **bank reconciliation, cash application and collections**: match records, investigate exceptions, identify the reason for a difference, document the result and improve the process where possible.

## Using Initiative

I try to be **proactive and self-motivated** and to identify what needs to be done rather than dealing only with the immediate problem.

At Mr Price, when an item had no clear pricing information, I checked the correct price for the customer and also corrected the missing shelf information so the same problem would be less likely to recur. I also became actively involved during a major store-floor reorganisation.

My independent project work reflects the same approach. I regularly work beyond formal course requirements when I think it will improve my understanding or the result.

For this role, that means being willing to **learn Cashbook’s systems quickly**, ask sensible questions, absorb feedback, investigate an application error methodically and keep working until I understand both the finance process and the technical cause.
