## Financial Mathematics | Why did you choose Financial Mathematics?

I chose Financial Mathematics because I enjoy **mathematics and problem solving**, but I wanted to apply those skills to something practical. Finance affects important decisions for businesses and their customers, so **accuracy, reliability and good judgement** really matter. I also like the customer-care side of financial services — using good analysis and attention to detail to provide a **professional, responsive service that clients can trust**.

**Recall:** maths → practical finance → customers → accuracy → trust
