**This is an interview workshop, not a set of scripts to memorise.** The questions help you recognise what employers are really assessing, connect each question to genuine experience, and practise giving a clear, natural answer. Choose a topic from the sidebar and explain it in your own words.

**Reflection is where the learning happens.** After each attempt, ask: *Did I answer the question? Did I give a real example? What would I change next time?* That cycle — **prepare, practise, reflect, improve** — helps you learn how to learn, and is useful long after the interview.
