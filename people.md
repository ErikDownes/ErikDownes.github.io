## STAR · Working well in a team

During my TY placement at Mr Price, I worked with a large team to reorganise the shop floor. We **planned the work**, but when staff became unavailable, I **changed tasks** to help where I was needed. We **finished on time** and kept disruption to customers to a minimum.

Planned the work|Changed tasks|Finished on time

## STAR · Explaining something clearly

My mother was comparing three PCP offers for the same car and was mainly looking at the monthly payments. I **listened to what mattered to her**, **compared the total costs** and **explained the difference simply**. She chose the middle offer and saved about €800.

Listened to what mattered to her|Compared the total costs|Explained the difference simply

## STAR · Handling a customer problem

At O’Mahony’s, outdated printed address information meant books were sent to the wrong library. I **helped organise the return**, **kept both libraries informed** and worked with colleagues to reduce the delay. Afterwards, I **raised the issue with my manager** so we could use more up-to-date information.

Helped organise the return|Kept both libraries informed|Raised the issue with my manager

## STAR · Handling a price complaint (illustrative example)

A customer was upset because the price at the till was higher than the shelf price. I **listened calmly**, then **checked the shelf label**. I **followed store policy** to sort out the difference and explained the outcome to the customer.

Listened calmly|Checked the shelf label|Followed store policy

## STAR · Adapting quickly

During a store reorganisation at Mr Price, some staff became unavailable and our plans had to change. I **checked the new priorities**, **moved between tasks** and **helped where I was needed**. We finished within the planned time, and I learned how important it is to be flexible.

Checked the new priorities|Moved between tasks|Helped where I was needed

## STAR · Responding to correction or direction

During my TY placement at Mr Price, my manager pointed out that I was returning late from breaks. I **accepted the feedback**, **changed my routine** and **made sure I was on time** afterwards. It was a useful lesson about respecting the team's time.

Accepted the feedback|Changed my routine|Made sure I was on time

## STAR · Pair programming with a classmate

In CE4702, I worked with one classmate on programming tasks. We **shared the work**, **checked each other's code** and **made sure we both understood** the final solution. That helped us work carefully and learn from each other.

Shared the work|Checked each other's code|Made sure we both understood
