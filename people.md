And the original job spec reinforces exactly the same idea: Pivotal talks about being **client focused and solutions driven**, adapting **quickly and efficiently**, and being **accessible, reliable, practical and professional at all times**. Pivotal Corporate Job Spec sem2…

So for your little **people/relationships journey**, I would use something like:

**from working independently to teamwork, client care and adding value**

Or slightly more Pivotal-flavoured:

**from individual work to teamwork, relationships and high-quality client service**

Or strongest of all for interview language:

**from individual achievement to teamwork, client care and adding value**

That last one is probably the best because it gives us a story arc:

**individual achievement → teamwork → client care → adding value**

And yes — next we can build **one concrete story under each of those four steps**, so it does not sound like borrowed corporate language.
