## Lease reporting | How would you approach preparing a report required by a Lease Agreement?

I would identify the required output and deadline, confirm the source data, work through the report systematically, check important figures and make sure it is ready early enough for review where required.

requirement | source data | deadline | check | review

## Problem solving | How do you approach a problem when you do not immediately know the answer?

I break the problem into smaller parts, identify what is known and unknown, make assumptions explicit and test the result. If I am still blocked, I ask a focused question explaining what I have already tried.

break down | assumptions | test | ask intelligently

## Difficult problem | Tell me about a difficult problem you solved.

I would choose an example where I had to diagnose the issue rather than simply follow instructions: what information I had, what options I considered, the method I chose and how I checked the outcome.

problem | method | validation | result

## Explain clearly | How would you explain something technical to a non-technical colleague?

I would first understand what the person needs to know, explain the main idea in plain language, introduce technical detail only where it helps, and check that the explanation has answered the actual question.

audience | purpose | plain language | detail | check

## Power BI | How would you approach systemising a recurring report?

I would first understand the existing process and the decisions the report supports. Then I would identify reliable data sources, standardise calculations and definitions, build a repeatable output, and validate the automated result against the existing process before relying on it.

process | data source | definitions | automation | validation

## Excel | Where would Excel fit in your workflow?

Excel is useful for quick inspection, reconciliation, calculations and communicating structured information. For larger or repeatable analysis I would also consider code or Power BI, but the choice should follow the task rather than the tool.
