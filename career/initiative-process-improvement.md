## Initiative | Give an example of how you would show initiative.

If I noticed a recurring problem or inefficient process, I would first understand why it was happening and then suggest a practical improvement. I would take initiative within my role while checking with others where a change could affect their work.

observe | understand | improve | consult

## Learning independently | Can you give an example of learning beyond your coursework?

We used **R for statistics** in college, but I wanted experience with Python as well because of its wider use in data analytics. I therefore recreated my statistical coursework in **both R and Python**, which also let me compare the approaches directly.

R | Python | self-directed learning | portfolio

## Process improvement | How would you improve a recurring process?

I would map the current steps, identify repetition, delays or error points, understand why the controls exist, and then propose the smallest change that makes the process clearer, faster or more reliable.

map | problem point | controls | improve | verify

## Automation | How would you decide whether a task should be automated?

I would look at how repetitive and predictable it is, the consequences of an error and whether human judgement is required. Good automation removes unnecessary work without removing appropriate oversight.

repetition | risk | judgement | oversight

## AI at work | How would you use AI as part of your work?

I would use AI to accelerate research, drafting, analysis or repetitive tasks where appropriate, while keeping responsibility for the final output. I would verify important facts and protect confidential information.

productivity | verification | confidentiality | responsibility

## Keeping skills relevant | How do you decide what to learn next?

I focus on durable skills while watching how tools used in industry are changing. I experiment with technology that has a practical application rather than trying to learn every new platform.

durable skills | industry awareness | experimentation | judgement
