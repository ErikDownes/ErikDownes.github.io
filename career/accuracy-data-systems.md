## Accuracy | How would you make sure your work was accurate?

I would work systematically, check important details before completing the task, and use available validation or review processes. For repeated work, I would prefer a consistent checking method rather than relying on memory.

systematic | check | validation | consistency

## Data entry | How would you approach entering or updating asset data?

I would first understand the required fields and source documents, enter the information consistently, check unusual values and confirm that the final record matches the source. I would avoid guessing where a value is unclear.

source | consistency | validation | no guessing

## New system | How would you learn unfamiliar software?

I would start with the guidance, get a simple task working, and then learn through use. I keep useful notes and ask focused questions when I reach something I cannot resolve myself.

guidance | practice | notes | focused questions

## Error | What would you do if you realised you had made an error?

I would identify the impact, correct it as quickly as possible, tell the appropriate person if it affects others, and review why it happened so that the process can be improved.

ownership | impact | correction | prevention

## Confidentiality | How would you handle confidential information?

I would only access or share information where there was a legitimate work reason, follow the organisation's procedures and avoid putting sensitive information into inappropriate systems or conversations.

need to know | procedure | privacy | judgement

## Data literacy | What does good data literacy mean in this role?

It means understanding what a field represents, where the data came from, whether it is complete and plausible, and how it will be used downstream in reporting, billing or decision-making.

meaning | source | quality | downstream use
