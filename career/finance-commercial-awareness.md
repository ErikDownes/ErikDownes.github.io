## Financial Mathematics | How does Financial Mathematics connect to aircraft leasing?

A leased aircraft produces contractual cash flows while retaining a future asset value. That creates direct links to present value, discounting, risk, financing, lease rates and residual value.

cash flows | discounting | risk | residual value

## Billing | Why does accurate billing support matter?

Billing converts contractual information into financial transactions. That means the underlying dates, rates, usage or other inputs need to be reliable, traceable and consistent with the agreement.

contract | inputs | accuracy | traceability

## Commercial judgement | What makes an aircraft commercially valuable?

Value depends on more than the purchase price. Relevant factors include lease income, customer quality, technical condition, market demand, remaining useful life, financing cost and expected residual value.

income | customer | condition | demand | residual value

## Cross-functional context | Why would Asset Management work with Commercial, Finance and Legal?

The same aircraft can create different questions for different teams. Commercial focuses on customer and placement decisions, Finance on cash flows and reporting, Legal on contractual rights and obligations, while Asset Management connects those perspectives to the aircraft and lease.

commercial | finance | legal | asset

## Assumptions | Why should assumptions be explicit in financial analysis?

A model can look precise while depending heavily on assumptions. I would make important assumptions visible, test how sensitive the result is to them, and separate measured data from estimates.
