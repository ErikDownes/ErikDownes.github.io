## STAR | How should I structure a competency example?

Use STAR briefly: set the context, explain your role, describe the actions you personally took, and finish with the result and what you learned.

**Working structure:** Situation → Task → Action → Result → learning.

## Communication practice | How do I prepare to communicate clearly?

The aim is not to memorise a speech. I prepare the evidence, pause before answering, make one clear point, support it, explain why it matters and stop.

**Working structure:** Pause → point → evidence → relevance → stop.

I also use AI as an iterative writing tool rather than as a substitute for judgement: draft, challenge, edit, verify and decide what I am willing to stand over.

## Learning approach | How do I learn unfamiliar material?

I learn best by engaging with the source, unpacking unfamiliar language, checking understanding, retrieving it later and then applying it to a harder or different problem. Feedback, self-assessment, peer review and publishing all create opportunities to find gaps and improve the work.

**Learning cycle:** Engage → unpack → practise → retrieve → apply → review → communicate → publish.
