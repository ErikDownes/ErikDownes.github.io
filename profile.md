I like taking mathematical ideas beyond the page: working with real data, testing assumptions, building models and turning the results into something that can be explored and explained clearly.

## What I work with

My main tools are **Python, Pandas, R, Excel, Jupyter/Google Colab, Java, SQL, Git/GitHub, Matplotlib, Plotly and Leaflet**.

My usual workflow is:

**question → data → cleaning → exploration → modelling → visualisation → communication**

I am especially interested in the point where analysis becomes useful to somebody else: a clear graph, an interactive map, a financial model, a calculator or a concise explanation.
