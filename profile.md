## How I Work

I want to become the person who can be trusted with a problem, not simply the person waiting to be shown the next step. That means working independently where I can, documenting what I do, checking assumptions and knowing when a question should be escalated rather than guessed.

I use technology when it improves the work: spreadsheets for quick inspection, code for reproducible analysis, GitHub for versioned work, and AI for research support, drafting and testing alternatives. **The tool can accelerate the work; responsibility for the work stays with me.**

My standard is simple: **understand enough to explain it, verify what matters, and leave the work in a form another person can follow.**

## Career Direction towards Asset Management

I am building toward analytical roles where **mathematics, finance, data and technology** are used to make practical decisions.

Aviation has always fascinated me. I still find it remarkable that we can take an aircraft weighing **tens of tonnes**, use mathematics, physics and engineering to generate lift, and routinely move hundreds of people across countries and continents. I studied Applied Mathematics, so ideas such as forces, motion and modelling already appealed to me, but I have also always enjoyed flying itself — particularly getting a window seat and watching how the aircraft, airport and wider system operate.

When this co-op opportunity arose, that general interest became much more focused. I started researching **regional aviation, turboprops, aircraft leasing and asset management**, looking at aircraft such as the **ATR 42, ATR 72 and Dash 8**, and trying to understand not just how an aircraft flies, but how it becomes a commercial asset: how it is financed, leased, maintained, monitored and ultimately returned or remarketed.

That combination is what attracts me. For co-op, I am particularly interested in **aviation, asset management, finance and analytics**. The common thread matters more than the job title: I want to work on real problems, learn from experienced people and become useful quickly.
