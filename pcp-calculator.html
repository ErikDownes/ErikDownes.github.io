## What the model is doing For each offer, the calculator treats the **GMFV as a balloon payment** at the end of the term. It calculates the monthly payment needed to amortise the amount financed down to that balloon, given the quoted APR. For the present-value comparison, every future monthly payment and the final GMFV are discounted back to today using the shared comparison discount rate: **PV cost = deposit + up-front fees + PV(monthly payments) + PV(final GMFV)** This is useful when offers have different deposits, monthly payments, terms and final payments. A low monthly payment is not automatically the cheapest offer. ## Important limitations This is a financial comparison model, not a replacement for the lender's legal quotation. Real PCP agreements may also differ in mileage limits, excess-mileage charges, damage standards, service plans, optional extras, settlement rules and other fees. Those should be considered before making a real decision.
