## Cycling

Cycling is personal first: fresh air, movement and time away from a screen. Strava can run quietly in the background, but the ride itself comes first.

It also creates an interesting dataset of distance, time, speed, elevation, route and milestones, so I have started using some of that data as a small analysis project.

## Cooking

Cooking is a much more regular part of my life. I enjoy experimenting with a meal, tasting the result and changing something the next time.

The page is deliberately more personal than technical: bolognese, roast chicken, the discovery of umami, eating out, and the simple feedback loop of trying to make the next version better.
