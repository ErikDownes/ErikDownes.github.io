Choose the questions that fit the conversation; there is no need to ask all of them.

## Fleet and options

**How do you think about the balance between aircraft already on lease and future purchase options?** As much as you can share, what time horizons matter most for deciding whether to exercise an option and place an aircraft with an airline?

**Follow-up if useful:** What is the average remaining lease term across the fleet, and how does that shape your planning?

## How deals develop

I notice airshows are often moments for announcements and meeting people across the industry. **How long does a typical aircraft acquisition or airline placement develop before an announcement?** What part do events such as airshows play in those relationships?

## The next twelve months

**What are Abelo's main priorities over the next year on the aircraft acquisition side and the airline customer side?** Are there particular regions or route needs you are watching most closely?

## Asset Management priorities

**What would you most like the Asset Management team to improve over the next year?** Where could an intern make a useful contribution to reporting, systems or day-to-day processes?

## Regional connectivity and ESG

**How does environmental, social and governance (ESG) thinking influence fleet decisions and the regions you serve?** Does the social value of regional connectivity enter the discussion alongside commercial returns?
