# Cashbook Limited — Implementation Associate (TIMS)

## Key Line

This is a **fintech implementation and customer-facing finance-process role** combining **bank reconciliation, cash application, collections, SQL, Excel, troubleshooting and automation**.

## Company

From the job specification, **Cashbook Limited** is an Irish software company based in the **Technology Park in Limerick** with customers internationally.

Its software automates finance processes including **Accounts Payable, Accounts Receivable and Bank Reconciliation**.

## Role

The placement is for an **Implementation Associate** on the **TIMS team**, reporting to the **TIMS Implementation Director** and working as a **junior business consultant**.

The work includes supporting implementations, learning cash application and collections, assisting with lockbox development, and working with customers to improve automation in bank reconciliation.

## Skills to Research

The strongest areas to understand before interview are:

- Bank reconciliation
- Cash application
- Collections
- Lockbox processing
- Accounts Payable and Accounts Receivable
- Microsoft SQL
- Excel
- Customer implementation and troubleshooting
- Finance-process automation
- AI tools in finance operations

## Interview Connection

This role is a strong match for transferable interview domains such as **communication, problem-solving, accuracy, customer care, organisation and learning new systems**.
