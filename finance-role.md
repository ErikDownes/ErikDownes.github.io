**FINANCE & ROLE = Cashbook · TIMS · bank reconciliation · cash application · collections · AP/AR.**

Use this domain when the question tests **basic finance knowledge or whether you understand the Cashbook Implementation Associate role**.

## Cashbook | What does Cashbook do?

Cashbook is a **financial-software company** that helps automate finance processes including **Accounts Payable, Accounts Receivable and Bank Reconciliation**.

## Role | What would you be doing as a TIMS Implementation Associate?

Support the TIMS implementation team, learn the finance processes, help with lockbox work, troubleshoot issues and work with customers to increase automation.

## Bank Reconciliation | What is bank reconciliation?

Compare the **bank record** with the organisation's own accounting records, identify differences and resolve or explain the exceptions.

## Cash Application | What is cash application?

Match incoming customer payments to the correct **customer accounts and invoices** so receivables are updated accurately.

## Collections | What are collections?

Follow up and manage **amounts owed by customers**, helping the business understand and reduce overdue receivables.

## TIMS | What does TIMS mean in this interview?

**Total Information Management System.** Cashbook integrates with TIMS to automate finance processes. For interview purposes, understand TIMS as the customer's wider business/ERP environment that Cashbook works with.
