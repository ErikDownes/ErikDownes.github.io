## What is the HSE Mid-West Co-Op Finance role?

This is a practical placement in the HSE Mid-West Finance Department, reporting to a **Finance Manager or assigned manager**. The responsibilities include month-end close, monthly **Pay / Non-Pay / Income** reconciliations, finance-policy compliance, Patient Level Costing and Activity Based Funding returns, private-insurer claims, AP payments through **SAP Financials**, deadlines and ad-hoc projects. It supports a public health service across Limerick, Clare and North Tipperary.

Responsibilities|Reporting|Service

## How does your work in finance support patients, even if you never see them?

Accurate finance information helps the organisation understand its costs, control expenditure, pay suppliers, prepare returns and allocate resources responsibly. The connection to patient services is **indirect but important**: reliable financial processes support the delivery of healthcare. I would not claim that a single spreadsheet directly determines patient care.

Financial stewardship|Resource use|Patient services

## Who would you report to and how would you learn the procedures?

I would report to the **Finance Manager** or another **assigned manager**. I would **learn the procedures**, **ask questions** when needed, **check my work thoroughly** and gradually **take more responsibility**.

Supervision|Learning|Responsibility

## What happens during month-end close?

Month-end close makes sure the financial records for the period are complete, accurate and suitable for reporting. I might help check transactions, identify missing information, investigate differences, prepare supporting schedules and pass issues to the responsible colleague. I would not post adjustments or approve transactions without authorisation.

Completeness|Controls|Reporting

## What is a financial reconciliation?

A reconciliation **compares two records that should agree**. First I check the period and source data; then match transactions, identify missing or duplicate entries, explain timing differences and investigate exceptions. The outcome is a supported balance or a clearly documented unresolved item—not a figure forced to agree.

Comparison|Investigation|Evidence

## What do Pay, Non-Pay and Income mean?

**Pay** covers employee-related expenditure, such as salaries. **Non-Pay** covers other operating expenditure, such as supplies and services. **Income** covers money received or receivable. For each category, monthly reconciliation means checking that the relevant transactions and balances match the underlying supporting information.

Pay|Non-Pay|Income

## Give an example of using Excel for a reconciliation.

Suppose an invoice register contains invoice IDs, amounts due and amounts paid. I could use **XLOOKUP** to match an invoice to a payment, **SUMIFS** to total payments by invoice, **IF / IFS** to flag Paid, Unpaid, Underpaid or Overpaid and filters to examine exceptions. I would then verify discrepancies against original records. A formula highlights a problem; it does not replace checking the evidence.

Matching|Exceptions|Verification

## What Excel skills would you bring to this job?

I am comfortable organising data into tables, sorting and filtering, using **SUM, SUMIFS, IF / IFS, XLOOKUP**, checking totals, and presenting clear results. I can use pivot tables for summaries and would test formula logic with known cases. I would be careful with dates, blanks, duplicate IDs and inconsistent formats because those are common reasons a spreadsheet gives a plausible but wrong result.

Data handling|Formulas|Validation

## What would you do if two figures did not agree?

I would **not alter a number merely to make the totals match**. I would check that I was comparing the same period, units and definitions; trace the relevant entries to the source; look for duplicates, omissions or timing differences; document what I found; and ask my manager when approval or further investigation was needed.

Investigation|Evidence|Escalation

## How would you check an Accounts Payable invoice?

I would check the supplier details, invoice number, date, amount and appropriate approval against the supporting documentation and the organisation's procedures. I would watch for duplicates or mismatches. Payment processing is controlled work, so I would follow the HSE's authorisation process rather than assuming I could approve a payment myself.

Verification|Approval|Controls

## What is SAP Financials?

**SAP Financials** is financial-management software used to record and process accounting transactions and support reporting. The job specifies **processing AP payments on SAP Financials**. I would not pretend to have used the HSE's SAP environment already; I would bring experience learning software, follow training and check each transaction carefully.

Purpose|Role use|Training

## What is Patient Level Costing?

Patient Level Costing analyses the cost of healthcare activity at the level of individual patients or episodes of care, using suitable costing methods and data. It helps the health service understand how resources are used. In this placement I might help validate, organise or reconcile supporting data for annual returns, under supervision.

Patient costs|Resource insight|Data reliability

## What is Activity Based Funding (ABF)?

**Activity Based Funding** uses information about the type and amount of healthcare activity, alongside applicable funding rules, to inform how services are funded. I would distinguish **activity data** from **cost data**, check that the reporting period and classifications were correct, and learn the specific return requirements rather than guessing at the methodology.

Activity|Funding|Data validation

## What are consultant private-insurer claims?

The role includes **collating consultant private-insurer claims for submission**. In practice, collating means collecting the required claim details and supporting documents, checking completeness, following privacy and financial controls, and preparing them for the authorised submission process. I would ask for the department's exact procedure before handling live claims.

Completeness|Confidentiality|Authorisation

## What does compliance with finance policies mean?

Finance policies set the rules for authorisation, recording, supporting evidence, handling data and auditability. I would work within those controls even when busy. If something was unclear or looked irregular, I would ask and escalate rather than improvise. Accuracy and confidentiality matter particularly when dealing with healthcare-related financial information.

Controls|Confidentiality|Escalation

## STAR · Demonstrating accuracy

An example I could use is checking information in a **library dispatch** task: a destination detail was wrong, so I helped arrange the correct return and communication rather than letting the error continue. I would describe **what I noticed, what I did and the outcome**, then connect the lesson to checking invoice or payment details before submission.

Attention to detail|Correction|Prevention

## STAR · Working well in a team

I could use the **Mr Price store reorganisation**: the team had to complete a large practical task, coordinate across shifts and adapt as priorities changed. I would explain **my particular contribution**, how I communicated with colleagues and how we kept the overall task moving. The link to HSE is being flexible and dependable in a busy service team.

Collaboration|Adaptability|Contribution

## STAR · Explaining something clearly

I could draw on **Java pair programming**: explain a coding decision to my partner, listen to their view, check that we both understood the implementation and integrate the solution. In finance, the same skill means explaining a discrepancy or spreadsheet finding clearly to a colleague who needs to act on it.

Clarity|Listening|Shared understanding

## STAR · Solving a technical problem

The **Dublin Bikes / Strava data project** is a useful example. Data from different sources may record time differently; I used Python and pandas to clean timestamps, consider time zones and align records so comparisons were meaningful. I would explain the original problem in plain English before introducing technical terms. This connects to validating data before producing finance reports.

Diagnosis|Method|Validation

## What would you do if you had three tasks and the same deadline?

I would clarify **urgency, dependency and importance**, identify any fixed reporting or payment deadlines, make a short plan and tell my manager early if there was a conflict. I would keep accurate work moving rather than rushing and silently making mistakes. I would update colleagues when a delay affected them.

Prioritisation|Communication|Quality

## What would you do if you made a mistake in a report?

I would disclose it promptly to the relevant person, establish **which figures or decisions might be affected**, correct it through the authorised process and document the change where necessary. I would then review the cause and put in a check to reduce the chance of recurrence.

Accountability|Correction|Prevention

## Why are you suited to HSE Mid-West Finance?

Financial Mathematics combines quantitative reasoning with computing, financial concepts and careful analysis. I can offer **accuracy, Excel and programming skills, teamwork and a willingness to learn**. I am particularly interested in practical finance processes—reconciliations, controls, reporting and systems—where disciplined checking makes an immediate difference.

Capability|Evidence|Motivation

## What would you ask the interviewer?

**What would a successful co-op student normally be able to do independently by the end of the placement?** I could also ask which parts of the month-end cycle and which Excel or SAP tasks I would learn first. These are questions about expectations and contribution, not promises about responsibilities I have not yet been given.

Expectations|Development|Contribution
