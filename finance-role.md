## What is the HSE Mid-West Co-Op Finance role?

It's a practical finance placement supporting month-end work, reconciliations, payments and reporting. I would work under the Finance Manager or assigned manager, learn the procedures and help make sure financial records are accurate and complete.

Responsibilities|Reporting|Service

## How does your work in finance support patients, even if you never see them?

Good finance work helps the HSE pay suppliers, control costs and make sound decisions about resources. It supports patient services indirectly by making the financial information dependable.

Financial stewardship|Resource use|Patient services

## Who would you report to and how would you learn the procedures?

I would report to the **Finance Manager or assigned manager**. I'd **listen carefully**, ask questions, **check my work** and gradually **take responsibility** as I learned the procedures.

Supervision|Learning|Responsibility

## What happens during month-end close?

The finance team checks that the month's transactions are complete and accurate before reporting. I could help with supporting records, reconciliations and investigating differences under supervision.

Completeness|Controls|Reporting

## What is a financial reconciliation?

A reconciliation checks whether two records agree. I would compare them, investigate missing or duplicated entries and explain any difference rather than changing a number just to make it match.

Comparison|Investigation|Evidence

## What do Pay, Non-Pay and Income mean?

**Pay** is staff-related expenditure, **Non-Pay** is other operating costs, and **Income** is money received or owed to the organisation. These are three areas the HSE specifically wants reconciled each month.

Pay|Non-Pay|Income

## Give an example of using Excel for a reconciliation.

I can match invoice references with **XLOOKUP**, total payments with **SUMIFS** and use **IF** to flag underpaid or overpaid items. Then I would check any exception against the original invoice and payment record.

Matching|Exceptions|Verification

## What Excel skills would you bring to this job?

I can organise, sort and filter data, use **IF**, **SUMIFS** and **XLOOKUP**, and summarise information with pivot tables. I'm strengthening these in a [practice workbook](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1CsGCxVfs8xBEGT7KsAcbAFXfqFl-XcvSe9Jb4rv5h0A/edit?gid=1524497728#gid=1524497728), always checking the results.

Data handling|Formulas|Validation

## What would you do if two figures did not agree?

I'd check that both figures cover the same period and source, then look for missing items, duplicates or timing differences. I'd document the issue and ask my manager before making a change requiring approval.

Investigation|Evidence|Escalation

## How would you check an Accounts Payable invoice?

I'd check the supplier, invoice number, amount and supporting documents, including the required approval. I'd watch for duplicates and follow the HSE's payment procedures before anything was processed.

Verification|Approval|Controls

## What is SAP Financials?

SAP Financials is a system for recording and processing finance transactions. The role uses it for Accounts Payable. I haven't used the HSE's system, but I would follow the training and check each step.

Purpose|Role use|Training

## What is Patient Level Costing?

Patient Level Costing looks at what individual patients or care episodes cost. In this placement I could help organise and check the data used for the required returns, under supervision.

Patient costs|Resource insight|Data reliability

## What is Activity Based Funding (ABF)?

Activity Based Funding connects funding with the type and volume of healthcare activity. I would help check the underlying data and learn the HSE's particular reporting rules rather than claim to know them already.

Activity|Funding|Data validation

## What are consultant private-insurer claims?

These are claims requiring information and documents to be gathered for submission to insurers. My role would be to check completeness, respect confidentiality and follow the authorised process.

Completeness|Confidentiality|Authorisation

## What does compliance with finance policies mean?

It means following the rules for approvals, accurate records, supporting documents and confidentiality. If something looks wrong or unclear, I would raise it rather than bypass a control to save time.

Controls|Confidentiality|Escalation

## STAR · Demonstrating accuracy

At O'Mahony's, outdated address information caused books to go to the wrong library. I helped organise the correction, kept the libraries informed and learned to check more current information before dispatch.

Attention to detail|Correction|Prevention

## STAR · Working well in a team

At Mr Price, our team reorganised the shop floor. When staffing changed, I switched tasks and helped where needed so the group could finish the work within the planned window.

Collaboration|Adaptability|Contribution

## STAR · Explaining something clearly

In Java pair programming, I talked through decisions with my partner and checked that we both understood the final code. I would use that same approach to explain a finance discrepancy clearly.

Clarity|Listening|Shared understanding

## STAR · Solving a technical problem

My Dublin Bikes files had inconsistent dates and timestamps. I learned how to standardise them with pandas, checked the results and then combined the data for analysis. The lesson was to validate data before trusting it.

Diagnosis|Method|Validation

## What would you do if you had three tasks and the same deadline?

I'd check which work was most urgent, what depended on other tasks and which deadlines were fixed. I'd make a plan and tell my manager early if the priorities conflicted.

Prioritisation|Communication|Quality

## What would you do if you made a mistake in a report?

I'd tell the relevant person quickly, check what the error affected and correct it through the proper process. Then I'd add a check to reduce the chance of repeating it.

Accountability|Correction|Prevention

## Why are you suited to HSE Mid-West Finance?

I enjoy finance, data and practical problem-solving. Financial Mathematics has developed my accuracy and analytical skills, while my work experience shows teamwork and responsibility. I'd bring those strengths and a willingness to learn the HSE's systems.

Capability|Evidence|Motivation

## What would you ask the interviewer?

What would a successful co-op student be able to handle independently by the end of the placement? I would also be interested in which Excel or SAP tasks I'd learn first.

Expectations|Development|Contribution
