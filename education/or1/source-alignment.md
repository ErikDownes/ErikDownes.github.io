## Archive | What was actually inspected

The supplied ZIP contains 89 files. After exact-duplicate removal and exclusion of unrelated material, the relevant academic set includes:

- lecture PDFs spanning the introduction, LP formulation, graphical solution, simplex, Big-M, Two-Phase, duality, sensitivity/dual simplex, transportation, MODI, dynamic programming, decision theory, classical optimisation, unconstrained optimisation, Lagrange multipliers and inequality-constrained optimisation;
- weekly tutorial sheets and several worked-solution files;
- a 2026 assignment and solutions;
- 2024 and 2025 examination and repeat-examination papers;
- exam-solution files and an exam-preparation guide;
- a one-slide module-information deck.

The ZIP also contains duplicate lecture/exam files and unrelated HSE, Strava, notebook, audio and website screenshots. Those are excluded from the academic course model.

## Assessment | Material in the archive

The module-information deck states:

- two lecture hours per week;
- one tutorial hour per week;
- continuous assessment worth 20%;
- final examination worth 80%.

The 2026 assignment covers graphical LP, Two-Phase, infeasibility, dynamic programming and transportation initial solutions.

The supplied exam-preparation guide explicitly highlights graphical LP, Big-M, dynamic programming, dual LP, decision theory, transportation including MODI, multivariable optimisation and KKT.

## Historical exams | Topics that vary by year

The 2024 examination archive contains goal programming and game theory in addition to graphical LP, Big-M and transportation. The 2025 papers emphasise graphical LP, Two-Phase/Big-M, duality, transportation, dynamic programming and decision environments.

For that reason **Goal Programming and Game Theory are retained as a labelled archive extension rather than silently presented as part of every year's lecture sequence.**

## Independence | How to remove the university wrapper later

University-specific references are concentrated in:

- the module wrapper front matter;
- this provenance page;
- the single course-home path in the course data file.

The lesson content, glossary, CSS and JavaScript use the independent name **Operations Research I**. To republish independently, change the home path/title and remove this page plus the module metadata; the learning content does not need to be rewritten.
