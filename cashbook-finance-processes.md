## What is cash application?

Cash application means matching money received from a customer to the correct customer account and invoice.

**Example:** €1,250 arrives from ACME and the reference says INV1042. Cash application links that payment to Invoice 1042.

## What is bank reconciliation?

Bank reconciliation means comparing the bank statement with the company's accounting records.

The aim is to explain every difference.

**Example:** the bank shows a €50 fee but the ledger does not. That is an item to investigate and record.

## What are collections?

Collections means following up money that customers still owe.

The finance team looks at overdue invoices, contacts customers and records reasons for non-payment such as disputes or short payments.

## What is an exception?

An exception is something the system cannot safely resolve automatically.

Instead of guessing, it sends the item for a person to review.
