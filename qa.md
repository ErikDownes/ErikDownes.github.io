## Why did you choose Financial Maths?



I chose Financial Mathematics because I have always enjoyed maths, right from primary school through secondary school. What really interested me was seeing how maths could be used to understand and solve real-world problems, especially through subjects like Physics and Applied Maths. Financial Maths felt like a natural next step because it let me continue with maths while also bringing in statistics, finance, accounting, business, data and computing. I became especially interested in computing when I saw how well it worked with maths and data. I also like the financial side because financial decisions have real consequences for people and organisations. They affect how people plan, invest, manage risk and make decisions, so there is a real responsibility to be accurate and get things right. For me, the course brings together the things I enjoy most: maths, technology, business and solving real problems.



**Breadcrumb:** enjoyed maths → real-world problems → natural next step → broad course → computing + data → finance matters → accuracy + responsibility → solving real problems



## Why are you interested in this role?

I’m interested in the role because it gives me a chance to apply what I have been learning in a real working environment. I like work where accuracy matters, where you have to understand the problem properly and where the result has a practical effect. I’m also interested in seeing how finance, data and technology are used together in a business. The role would let me develop those skills while learning from people who are already working in the area.

**Breadcrumb:** apply learning → real work → accuracy → practical effect → finance + data + technology → learn from others

## What are your main strengths?

I would say my main strengths are that I am analytical, accurate and willing to learn. I like breaking a problem down and working through it carefully rather than rushing to an answer. I am also comfortable with numbers, data and technology, and I have developed that through both my degree and my own projects. I work well with other people, but I am also happy to take responsibility for a task and make sure it is finished properly.

**Breadcrumb:** analytical → accurate → break problems down → numbers + data + technology → teamwork → responsibility

## Why should we choose you for this placement?

I think I would bring a strong combination of maths, finance and technology, but also a willingness to learn. I would not expect to know everything when I start, but I would expect myself to listen, ask good questions and improve quickly. I am comfortable working carefully with data and financial information, and I understand that accuracy and reliability matter in this kind of work. I would also bring a positive attitude and I would be willing to contribute wherever the team needed me.

**Breadcrumb:** maths + finance + technology → willing to learn → listen + ask → improve quickly → accuracy + reliability → contribute to team
