# Questions

## Growth | How far does Abelo want to grow?

**When you think about growth, is the strategy essentially to keep expanding within the turboprop market, or are there circumstances where Abelo would consider moving into other aircraft types?**

Possible follow-up:

**Is there a point where growing the fleet too quickly would actually increase risk rather than create value?**

### What they might say

Abelo may see more value in **growing depth rather than breadth** — adding aircraft, customers and geographic reach while retaining specialist expertise in regional turboprops.

They may also talk about:

- airline demand;
- aircraft availability;
- funding capacity;
- concentration risk;
- residual values;
- management capacity;
- staying within an asset class they understand extremely well.

## Asset Management | Where does analysis become a decision?

**Where in Asset Management would I most often see data or financial analysis turn into an actual commercial decision?**

Possible follow-up:

**Would that tend to happen around leases, billing, aircraft transitions, forecasting or something else?**

### What they might say

They may point to:

- lease reporting;
- billing;
- aircraft utilisation;
- maintenance information;
- aircraft transitions;
- lease extensions or amendments;
- forecasting;
- supporting Commercial, Finance, Legal or Technical teams;
- management reporting.

This is one of the strongest questions for Erik because it directly connects his **Financial Mathematics and data background** to the work of the placement.

## Options | How do you commit without overcommitting?

**When Abelo takes options on future ATR deliveries, how do you balance securing aircraft early against not knowing exactly what demand will look like at delivery?**

### What they might say

Options can give Abelo **capacity and flexibility**.

They may talk about:

- securing future production slots;
- prospective airline customers;
- market forecasts;
- aircraft values;
- financing conditions;
- timing;
- deciding when an option becomes attractive enough to exercise.

The key trade-off is between **securing future aircraft** and avoiding excessive commitments before demand is clear.

## Risk | Geopolitical and Financial Risk

**With geopolitical disruption, sanctions and large movements in interest rates and inflation, how does Abelo protect aircraft values and lease returns against those risks?**

### What they might say

They may discuss:

- geographic diversification;
- airline credit assessment;
- sanctions compliance;
- insurance;
- lease protections;
- aircraft mobility and repossession risk;
- interest-rate exposure;
- inflation;
- residual-value assumptions;
- avoiding excessive exposure to one airline, country or region.

## Location | Limerick and Dublin

**How is the Asset Management team split between Limerick and Dublin, and would the placement involve much travel between the two offices?**

Possible follow-up:

**Would Dublin normally mean occasional meetings or a day or two at a time, rather than spending extended periods based there?**

### What they might say

They may explain:

- where Erik’s immediate team is based;
- how often colleagues travel between the offices;
- whether meetings or training take place in Dublin;
- whether particular projects require short stays;
- how much work is done remotely versus in person.

This is a useful practical question because occasional travel to Dublin is very different from being expected to spend long periods based there.

## Airshows | What actually happens there?

**I haven’t been to a major airshow myself, but I’ve noticed how much business seems to happen around events like Paris and Dubai. Is Abelo planning to attend either in 2027, and what kind of team normally represents the company?**

Possible follow-up:

**Is it mainly senior management and Commercial, or do teams such as Asset Management, Finance, Technical or marketing also get involved?**

### What they might say

Major airshows bring together:

- manufacturers;
- airlines;
- lessors;
- financiers;
- suppliers;
- governments;
- media.

Senior management and Commercial are likely to be central, while other functions may contribute depending on the meetings, transactions or announcements involved.

Aircraft deals announced at airshows may have been discussed or negotiated well before the event. The airshow gives companies a concentrated opportunity for meetings, relationship-building and publicity.

If the conversation is relaxed, Erik can finish with:

**So Dublin now and again is fine — just reassure me you’re not sending the intern to Paris or Dubai.**
