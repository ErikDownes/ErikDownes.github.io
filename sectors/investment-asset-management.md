## What the sector involves

Investment and asset-management roles analyse assets, cash flows, expected returns, uncertainty, performance and risk. The asset might be a security, fund, property, loan or other investment.

## Degree connections

Relevant study includes [Finance]({{ '/modules/fi4003-finance.html' | relative_url }}), [Mathematical and Statistical Models of Investments]({{ '/modules/ms4528-mathematical-and-statistical-models-of-investments.html' | relative_url }}), Financial Mathematics, statistics and stochastic modelling.

## Evidence

The strongest portfolio evidence is work that makes assumptions explicit, models cash flows or value, tests sensitivity and communicates the result clearly.
