## What the sector involves

Data, analytics and FinTech roles use software and quantitative methods to clean data, automate workflows, build models, create reporting systems and support financial decisions.

## Degree connections

Relevant study includes Applied Data Analysis, probability and statistics, numerical analysis, computer software, operations research and financial mathematics.
