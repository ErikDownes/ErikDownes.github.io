## What the sector involves

Banking and financial services use quantitative analysis to support lending, credit decisions, treasury, risk, pricing, forecasting, reporting and customer or corporate finance.

## Degree connections

Useful foundations include [Finance]({{ '/modules/fi4003-finance.html' | relative_url }}), [Fundamentals of Financial Mathematics]({{ '/modules/ms4027-fundamentals-of-financial-mathematics.html' | relative_url }}), probability, statistics, numerical analysis and operations research.

## Evidence

Financial calculators, data-analysis projects and structured comparison of cash flows provide practical examples of turning quantitative work into a decision.
