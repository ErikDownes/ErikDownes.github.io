## What the sector does

Corporate service providers help organisations and investors establish and operate companies and transaction structures. Typical work includes:

- company and SPV administration,
- bookkeeping, journals and reconciliations,
- accounts payable and receivable,
- financial and regulatory reporting,
- cash and transaction administration,
- company-secretarial and governance work,
- KYC, CDD and beneficial-ownership checks,
- document management, deadlines and client communication.

A useful distinction is **asset versus structure**. An aircraft lessor may focus on the aircraft itself — condition, maintenance, utilisation, lease terms and residual value. A corporate services team may instead focus on the entity that owns it, the cash flows, records, filings and governance around the transaction.

## Core concepts

**SPV** — a separate legal entity created for a defined transaction or purpose.

**Reconciliation** — comparing two records or balances and investigating differences.

**Journal** — an accounting entry recording a transaction.

**Accounts payable / receivable** — money the entity owes / money owed to the entity.

**KYC / CDD** — identifying clients, ownership, control and relevant risk information.

**Beneficial ownership** — identifying the person who ultimately owns or controls an entity.

**Company secretarial work** — statutory records, filings, board documentation and governance administration.

**Internal controls** — checks designed to reduce errors, omissions and inappropriate transactions.
