## What the sector involves

Insurance and actuarial work is built around uncertainty: estimating frequency and severity of future events, pricing risk, setting reserves, testing assumptions and communicating the limits of a model.

## Degree connections

Strong links include [Probability Models]({{ '/modules/ms4035-probability-models.html' | relative_url }}), [Statistical Inference]({{ '/modules/ms4214-statistical-inference.html' | relative_url }}), [Stochastic Processes]({{ '/modules/ms4217-stochastic-processes.html' | relative_url }}) and Financial Mathematics.

## Evidence

Projects involving uncertain data, validation, modelling and sensitivity analysis are particularly relevant because the quality of the assumptions matters as much as the calculation itself.
