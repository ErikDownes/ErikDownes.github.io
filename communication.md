## Explain Clearly | Tell me about a time you explained something complex clearly.

My mother was comparing three PCP offers for the same car. I built a calculator to compare the deposit, monthly repayments, final payment and total cash outlay, then used simple figures and graphs to explain the result. The middle option was about €800 cheaper overall.

## Customer Communication | Tell me about a time communication helped resolve a customer problem.

At O’Mahony’s, books were sent to the wrong library because the address information we were working from was incorrect. I helped organise the return, kept the libraries informed and worked with colleagues to minimise the delay. The important part was being clear about what had happened, what we were doing next and when the customer could expect it to be resolved.

Listen|Explain|Update|Resolve

## Adapt to Audience | How do you adapt your communication to different audiences?

I start with what the other person actually needs. A customer usually needs a clear practical explanation and next step, while a colleague may need more operational detail. For technical work I use the format that makes the information easiest to understand — for example a short written summary, a structured table, a graph or a flow diagram — and I adjust my pace and level of detail as I go.

Audience|Purpose|Detail|Format

## Feedback | Tell me about a time you received corrective feedback.

Early in my TY placement at Mr Price, my manager corrected me on punctuality after breaks; I accepted it, understood the effect on the team, and changed my routine immediately.

Listen|Accept|Understand|Change

## Written Communication | How do you make technical or written information clear and accessible?

I use clear headings, concise explanations, properly structured tables and visualisations where they genuinely help. In project work I have used Overleaf and LaTeX for structured reports and GitHub Pages for publishing technical work so that another person can follow the purpose, method and result without having to work through the raw code first.

Structure|Clarity|Visuals|Audience
