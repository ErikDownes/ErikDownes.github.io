# HSE Finance Interview

## Key Line

This is a **hands-on finance placement** involving month-end work, reconciliations, controls, costing and funding returns, AP payments, SAP Financials and Excel.

## What is the role?

It is a **Co-Op Finance role in the HSE Mid-West Finance Department**, supporting day-to-day financial work and reporting to a **Finance Manager or assigned manager**.

## What finance work would you be doing?

The main areas are **month-end close, Pay/Non-Pay/Income reconciliations, finance-policy compliance, patient-level costing and activity-based funding returns, insurer claims and AP payments on SAP Financials**.

## Why are you suited to it?

My Financial Mathematics background gives me a strong base in **numbers, accuracy, analysis and problem solving**. I also like structured work where information has to be checked, reconciled and completed properly.

## How would you approach a reconciliation?

I would compare the relevant records, identify any differences, trace them back to the source information and either **resolve or escalate the exception** rather than forcing the figures to agree.

## What would you bring to the team?

I would bring **accuracy, initiative, flexibility and a willingness to learn**. I would expect to learn the HSE's systems and procedures properly, then become useful to the team as quickly as possible.

## What should you ask them?

A useful question is: **What would a co-op student normally become able to handle independently during the placement?**
