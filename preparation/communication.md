## Communication | Tell me about a time you communicated something clearly

When I realised a parcel had been sent to the wrong library, I immediately recognised that there were **two customers to take care of**. One customer was expecting the correct delivery and needed to know there would be a short delay. The other had received the parcel by mistake.

I explained the situation clearly to both. I also suggested that, rather than arranging a costly return, we let the receiving library keep the books free of charge. My manager agreed. It solved the problem efficiently, maintained goodwill and kept both customers informed.

**Recall:** two customers → explain quickly → practical solution → goodwill

## Client Service | What does good customer care mean to you?

Good customer care means being **responsive, reliable and professional**. It starts with listening properly, understanding what the customer needs and then following through on what you say you will do.

Being accurate matters, but so does making the customer feel that their issue is being taken seriously.

**Recall:** listen → understand → respond → follow through

## Explain Clearly | How would you explain something technical to a non-technical person?

I would first work out what the person actually needs to know. Then I would explain the main idea in plain language, use a practical example if helpful, and only add technical detail when it improves understanding.

Finally, I would check that the explanation made sense to them.

**Recall:** audience → main point → plain language → example → check
