## Why the work interests me

What appeals to me about asset management is how many things come together: the aircraft, the airline, the lease, the finances and the information behind each decision. I enjoy finding patterns and logic in problems that seem complicated at first. Here, the inputs are not just numbers. Relationships, contracts, regulation and wider economic changes matter too.

I have always been fascinated by aircraft. Preparing for this role has helped me see one as both an engineering asset and a financial asset. Abelo's focus on regional aircraft makes that connection especially interesting to me.

## What I have explored

Abelo's published figure of 61 aircraft made me curious about the fleet. I built an interactive dashboard using public data to investigate the aircraft and where they operate. Working through gaps and inconsistencies in the sources taught me to check what a result really means. I would be happy to show a quick example.

## What I would bring

I know the internship also involves careful day-to-day work. Reports must be accurate and ready within the timeframes in lease agreements; data in internal systems and billing information need the same attention. I would take those responsibilities seriously, communicate early if something is unclear and learn from the people who know the business.

I use Excel and other data tools, and I would like to develop my Power BI skills in the team's reporting environment. The opportunity to work with Asset Management, Commercial, Finance and Legal—and to help make repeated reporting clearer and more reliable—is exactly the kind of co-op experience I am looking for.
