# Asset Management

# Asset Management

## Key Line

**Asset management is about keeping the aircraft flying, earning money and protecting its value.**

## What is Asset Management?

An aircraft is a valuable asset.

The asset manager helps make sure it is:

- **operating**
- **properly maintained**
- **correctly recorded**
- **retaining its value**

## Where does it sit?

**Buy → Lease → Manage → Re-lease or Sell**

The aircraft may move between airlines, but its **maintenance history and records must move with it**.

## What do you track?

Remember just four things:

**Hours → Cycles → Maintenance → Records**

- **Flight hours** = time flying
- **Flight cycle** = one take-off and landing
- **Maintenance** = what is due and when
- **Records** = proof of the aircraft's history

Regional aircraft can fly **six or eight short flights a day**, so they can build up cycles quickly.

## Why does it matter financially?

More flying means more maintenance will eventually be needed.

That affects:

**Maintenance → Cost → Aircraft Value**

Maintenance reserves help put money aside for major future maintenance.

## Interview Answer

**“My understanding is that aircraft asset management is about keeping the aircraft operating and earning while protecting its value. You track things like flight hours, cycles, maintenance condition and technical records. That is especially important with regional aircraft because they can do several take-offs and landings every day. All of that affects future maintenance costs and ultimately the value of the aircraft.”**
