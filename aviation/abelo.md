# What do you know about Abelo?

Abelo was formed in 2022 by bringing together Elix Aviation, an aircraft lessor, and ADARE Aviation Capital, an asset manager, combining the commercial and technical sides of aircraft ownership.

Their focus is deliberately on turboprops, which are ideal for regional, short-haul and lower-density routes. Rather than trying to cover every type of aircraft, they’ve stayed specialist, building expertise across leasing and asset management, which really go hand in hand. They’ve also continued to strengthen their relationship with ATR in Toulouse.

As part of my preparation, I built an interactive dashboard using public ATR and Dash 8 data. When I compared it with Abelo’s published figures, it came out almost exactly the same — 61 aircraft, 26 lessees and 19 countries.

There are five aircraft where I still haven’t identified the lessee, which is actually a good example of the kind of real-world data problem you get.

I think that’s quite close to the type of work I could bring to the role — checking data, organising it and turning it into useful reporting. I have the dashboard here on my laptop if you’d like to see it.
