## What do you know about aircraft leasing?

An airline can buy an aircraft or lease one. Aircraft leasing has strong roots close to home: Guinness Peat Aviation was founded in Shannon in 1975 and helped build the industry in Ireland.

For the airline, leasing provides aircraft capacity without the full upfront cost of buying. It can put the aircraft to work, earn revenue and make regular lease payments. It also has certainty about that aircraft for the agreed lease term.

For the lessor, those payments provide a source of income, but the return has to be weighed against the cost of financing the aircraft and the risks involved. The aircraft is a valuable asset, so its maintenance, condition and records matter throughout the lease.
