## What do you know about aircraft leasing?

An airline can either buy or lease an aircraft. Modern aircraft leasing really took off with Tony Ryan and GPA in Shannon in the 1970s.



According to Aircraft Leasing Ireland in 2025, 41% of the world’s aircraft are leased. And 64% of those are managed from Ireland. So roughly a quarter of the world’s aircraft are managed by Irish-based leasing firms.



The airline, or lessee, gets access to aircraft without the huge upfront capital investment. They can start flying passengers, generating revenue, paying their costs and making a profit. They get capacity certainty for the duration of the lease.



For the lessor, the aircraft is an investment that generates lease income. But they also have to protect that investment. Maintenance, inspections and records have to be managed throughout the lease to protect the aircraft and its value.
