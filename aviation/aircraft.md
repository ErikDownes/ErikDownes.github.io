## Turboprops | Tell the story of aeroplanes from first powered flight to turboprops to jets?

The first powered aircraft, including the **Wright Flyer**, used **piston engines driving propellers**.

As aviation technology developed, piston engines were increasingly supplemented by **gas-turbine engines**. This produced two important paths: **jet engines**, which create thrust directly, and **turboprops**, where a turbine engine drives a propeller.

Jets became dominant for **high-speed and longer-distance travel**. Turboprops remained particularly effective on **shorter regional routes**, where the time advantage of a jet is smaller and fuel efficiency, operating cost and airport flexibility become more important.

Modern regional aircraft such as the **ATR 42, ATR 72 and De Havilland Canada Dash 8** use turboprop engines because they are well suited to frequent short sectors between regional airports.

**Remember:** **piston propeller → gas turbine → jet for speed, turboprop for efficient regional flying.**
