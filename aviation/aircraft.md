## Tell the story of aeroplanes from the  first powered flight to turboprops and jets?

Powered flight began with the Wright brothers, who used piston engines driving propellers.

The gas-turbine engine was eventually developed, giving rise to turboprops and jet engines.

Turboprops are most effective on shorter regional routes, while jets became dominant for high-speed and longer-distance travel.

Your modern turboprop fleet is focused on the French-Italian ATR 42 and ATR 72, while you continue to lease Dash 8 aircraft inherited from the Elix portfolio.
