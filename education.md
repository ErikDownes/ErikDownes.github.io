## Why these areas matter

The grouping is deliberately different from the semester structure. Employers rarely ask whether a topic appeared in Semester 2 or Semester 4; they ask whether the student can work with **data, uncertainty, finance, software, optimisation, modelling and evidence**.

The same module can therefore contribute to more than one professional capability. For example, Applied Data Analysis belongs academically with statistics, but it also contributes to computing, data quality, communication and decision support.

- [Skills Profile]({{ '/skills-profile.html' | relative_url }}) — quantitative, technical and workplace capabilities.

- [Sectors]({{ '/sectors.html' | relative_url }}) — connect modules and skills to industries, roles and company case studies such as Pivotal Corporate and Abelo.
