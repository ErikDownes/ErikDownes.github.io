<h2>Question 2</h2>
<div class="ms4045-math-click" markdown="1">

\[
\text{(a)}\quad \frac{1+3i}{3-i},
\qquad
\text{(b}^+\text{)}\quad \frac{3-i}{1+3i}.
\]
</div>
</section>

<section class="ms4045-solution-stage" aria-label="Worked solution">
<div class="ms4045-solution-head">
<strong>Worked solution</strong>
<span>Tap below for the next step · tap the question maths above to show all</span>
</div>
<div class="ms4045-solution-body" markdown="1">
<div class="ms4045-rule"><strong>Method:</strong> multiply top and bottom by the conjugate of the denominator. The denominator then becomes a real number because \((a+ib)(a-ib)=a^2+b^2\).</div>

**(a)**
\[
\frac{1+3i}{3-i}
=
\frac{(1+3i)(3+i)}{(3-i)(3+i)}
=
\frac{3+i+9i+3i^2}{10}
=
\frac{10i}{10}
=
\boxed{i}.
\]

**(b)**
\[
\frac{3-i}{1+3i}
=
\frac{(3-i)(1-3i)}{(1+3i)(1-3i)}
=
\frac{3-9i-i+3i^2}{10}
=
\frac{-10i}{10}
=
\boxed{-i}.
\]
</div>
