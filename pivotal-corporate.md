## Company | What does Pivotal Corporate do?

Pivotal Corporate is an independently owned **Corporate Service Provider (CSP)** with more than 100 professionals across Shannon, Dublin, London and New York.

Its work sits behind companies, transactions and investment structures. Clients may need an entity formed, accounts maintained, board and company-secretarial work organised, cash movements administered, regulatory requirements met, or an international operation supported. Pivotal provides the people and systems that keep that work accurate, compliant and moving.

Its services include:

- company formation and company management,
- financial accounting and financial reporting support,
- company secretarial and governance services,
- transaction and cash management,
- compliance, tax and regulatory support,
- international expansion and restructuring,
- managing-agent services and staff augmentation.

The company works across sectors including aviation, private equity, real estate, private debt, structured debt, infrastructure, technology and pharmaceuticals.

## Business model | What are clients actually paying for?

A client is not only paying for a set of administrative tasks. They are paying for **reliable execution**.

That means accurate books and records, deadlines met, documents complete, queries answered, transactions progressed and issues identified early enough to deal with them.

That helps explain why Pivotal places so much emphasis on **Experience, Responsiveness and Delivery**. Those words describe a practical service model:

**Experience** — understand the transaction, rules and client context.  
**Responsiveness** — deal with questions and problems quickly and clearly.  
**Delivery** — complete the work accurately and on time.

The company also describes its approach as solutions-driven, hands-on and collaborative, with the aim of becoming a long-term partner of choice for clients.

## Shannon | Why does the location stand out?

One thing I find interesting is that significant international corporate and financial-services work is being delivered from Shannon as well as Dublin, London and New York.

That challenges the idea that this type of work has to be concentrated in a major financial centre. It also shows the strength of the wider Shannon and Limerick business ecosystem, particularly where international investment, aviation, finance and professional services overlap.

For me, that makes the company more interesting rather than less international: the work can be locally based while the clients, transactions and relationships are global.

## Role | What would I actually be doing?

The Co-op Student / Corporate Administrator role combines finance, accounting, administration and compliance support for international clients.

The specification includes work such as journals and reconciliations, accounts payable and receivable, maintaining financial records, VAT and tax or regulatory reporting, company administration, legal and regulatory documentation, KYC/CDD support, research and process improvement.

The common thread is dependable information: transactions have to be recorded correctly, supporting documents have to be complete, deadlines matter and the work must stand up to review.

## Communication | Make the information useful

Good communication is not about giving everybody the same explanation. It is about knowing the audience and deciding what they actually need in order to understand something or make a decision.

A useful example is a PCP comparison I worked through for a family member. Three finance structures were all feasible: a larger deposit with lower monthly repayments, the reverse, and an intermediate option. Looking only at the deposit or monthly payment could easily bias the decision.

I calculated the overall costs and graphed the alternatives. The visual made the useful conclusion obvious: two options were effectively the same, while one was about **€800 cheaper**.

The calculation mattered, but so did the presentation. The lesson for me was that **good analysis becomes useful when the person receiving it can understand and act on it**.

## Client service | Professional without being distant

I think good service requires a balance. The work has to be accurate, reliable and professional, but the person on the other side should also find you approachable and easy to deal with.

That means listening properly, giving a clear answer, following through on what you said you would do and adjusting the level of detail to the person you are speaking to.

For a corporate-services business, that seems particularly important because many client relationships continue across repeated transactions and over long periods of time.

**SPV** — a separate company created for a specific transaction or purpose.  
**KYC / CDD** — identifying the client, ownership and relevant risk information.  
**Reconciliation** — comparing two records and explaining any difference.  
**Journal** — an accounting entry used to record a transaction.  
**AP / AR** — money the business owes / money owed to the business.  
**Beneficial ownership** — identifying the person who ultimately owns or controls an entity.  
**Company secretarial** — statutory filings, company records, board documents and governance administration.  
**Internal controls** — checks designed to reduce errors, omissions and inappropriate transactions.
