## Company | What does Pivotal Corporate do?

Pivotal Corporate is an independently owned Corporate Service Provider. Its work includes company formation and management, financial accounting, company secretarial services, transaction and cash management, regulatory and tax reporting, and support for international corporate structures.

The company says it has more than 100 staff across Shannon, Dublin, London and New York and works across sectors including aviation, private equity, real estate, private debt, structured debt, infrastructure, technology and pharmaceuticals.

## Role | What would I actually be doing?

The Co-op Student / Corporate Administrator role combines finance, accounting, administration and compliance support for international clients.

The specification includes work such as journals and reconciliations, accounts payable and receivable, maintaining financial records, VAT and tax or regulatory reporting, company administration, legal and regulatory documentation, KYC/CDD support, research and process improvement.

The common thread is dependable information: transactions have to be recorded correctly, supporting documents have to be complete, deadlines matter and the work must stand up to review.

## Fit | Why does Financial Maths fit this role?

Financial Mathematics gives me a strong base in quantitative reasoning, finance, probability, statistics and structured problem solving. That is useful in a role where accuracy, reconciliations, financial records and careful checking matter.

I also have practical experience with Excel, Python and data cleaning. The tools may be different in the workplace, but the underlying habits are similar: understand the data, check it, investigate differences and make the final output clear.

My work experience adds a different side. At O'Mahony's I have dealt with orders, invoices and dispatch, where accuracy and communication affect the next stage of the process. At Mr Price I developed confidence dealing with customers and resolving everyday problems.

**SPV** — a separate company created for a specific transaction or purpose.  
**KYC / CDD** — identifying the client, ownership and relevant risk information.  
**Reconciliation** — comparing two records and explaining any difference.  
**Journal** — an accounting entry used to record a transaction.  
**AP / AR** — money the business owes / money owed to the business.  
**Beneficial ownership** — identifying the person who ultimately owns or controls an entity.  
**Company secretarial** — statutory filings, company records, board documents and governance administration.  
**Internal controls** — checks designed to reduce errors, omissions and inappropriate transactions.

## Concepts | What should I understand before the interview?

**Journal** — a formal accounting entry recording a transaction.

**Reconciliation** — comparing two records or balances and investigating any difference.

**Accounts payable / receivable** — money the business owes / money owed to the business.

**VAT and regulatory reporting** — preparing accurate information for statutory or regulatory requirements.

**KYC / CDD** — Know Your Customer / Customer Due Diligence: identifying clients and understanding relevant ownership, control and risk information.

**SPV** — Special Purpose Vehicle: a separate legal entity created for a defined transaction or purpose.

**Company secretarial work** — maintaining statutory records, filings, board documentation and corporate-governance requirements.
