# Questions to show you know the role

## Who is Pivotal Corporate?

Pivotal Corporate is an **independent, solutions-driven corporate services firm** that helps **US-based investors establish and grow their businesses in Europe**.

## How does Pivotal support clients?

Pivotal supports clients **at every stage**, from the **initial assessment** through to becoming **fully operational**, using its local networks across different jurisdictions.

## What does client-focused mean?

It means **adapting quickly to each client’s changing needs** and helping them focus on **running and growing their core business**.

## What sets Pivotal apart?

**Delivery.** Pivotal aims to be **responsive, accessible, reliable, practical and professional**.

## What is the role?

The role supports the **Client Service Teams** in providing **accounting and administration services** and maintaining a **high-quality professional service**.



&nbsp;

## What is Company Secretarial?

**Company Secretarial is an internal function within Pivotal** that supports client companies with **corporate governance, statutory records, company filings, board paperwork and other compliance requirements**.

It is different from an ordinary secretary or administrator in the client’s own company.

## Job Description  Original

### Pivotal Corporate – Co-Op Student/Corporate Administrator

Pivotal Corporate is an independent, solutions driven firm with extensive experience assisting US based investors to establish and grow their European investments. We partner with our clients at every stage in the process using our local networks in a number of jurisdictions to ensure their project runs smoothly from the initial assessment phase through to the project being fully operational.

With our client focused and solutions driven delivery, we have the ability to adapt to the ever-changing needs of our clients quickly and efficiently, becoming the pivot point for their business and allowing them to concentrate on their core business needs as they grow.

Delivery is what sets us apart from other firms. Our clients appreciate our unrivalled responsiveness and our passion and dedication. We pride ourselves on being accessible, reliable, practical, and professional at all times.

We are currently seeking to hire a pro-active, self-motivated, and highly organised student to join the business. This role will support our Client Service Teams in providing Accounting & Administration services to a portfolio of clients, ensuring that our clients are provided with a high-quality professional service.

### Duties and Responsibilities:

- To develop a basic understanding of the mechanics of company administration and develop business like relationships with intermediaries and internal clients with whom you will come into regular contact.
- Demonstrate a willingness to apply high standards of client care to all dealings with intermediaries and internal clients.
- Duties as set out by senior members of staff including (but not limited to):
- Support the processing of journal entries and account reconciliations.
- Assist with accounts payable and accounts receivable activities
- Assist with VAT, tax and regulatory reporting processes
- Help maintain accurate financial records and supporting documentation
- Support compliance with company policies and accounting standards
- Support special projects and process improvement initiatives.
- Conduct research into financial, accounting and market topics as required.
- Participate in team meetings and contribute ideas for operational improvement
- Deal with day-to-day administration tasks assigned by company secretarial group including (but not limited to):
- Maintenance of internal schedules and registers;
- Process KYC and CDD requirement to ensure compliance with statutory requirements; and
- Organise and facilitate for the signing of regulatory and legal documentation.

### Job Specific Requirements:

- Organised, detail-oriented individual with well-developed critical thinking skills and possesses the ability to manage multiple tasks in a fast-paced environment.
- Strong written and verbal skills.
- Working knowledge of Microsoft Word, Excel, and Outlook.
- A positive attitude, demonstrating enthusiasm and the desire to take on responsibility. A willingness to get involved in team issues and events.
- An interest in financial services, and a genuine desire to learn how this can be used as a tool for adding value to a client’s business.

## What is a reconciliation? | Compare · Explain · Resolve

**Remember:** compare · explain · resolve

A reconciliation compares two records or balances that should agree. If they do not, the important part is not forcing them to match; it is identifying and explaining every difference.

I would check the source records, amounts, dates and references, identify timing differences or errors, correct what should be corrected and escalate anything I could not resolve.

That kind of work suits me because I naturally like tracing discrepancies rather than ignoring them.

## What are accounts payable and receivable? | Owe · Owed · Control

**Remember:** owe · owed · control

Accounts payable is money the company owes to suppliers or other parties. Accounts receivable is money owed to the company.

Both require accurate invoices and records, correct dates and references, monitoring of outstanding balances and follow-up where something does not match or has not been paid.

My logistics work gives me a practical connection to invoices, orders and checking that information agrees before something moves to the next stage.

## Give an example of attention to detail | Orders · Check · Resolve

**Remember:** orders · check · resolve

At O’Mahony’s, books and other items had to be matched to the correct orders, invoices, box counts and destinations before dispatch.

A small error could mean the wrong material going to a school or customer, so I learned to check the information rather than assuming it was right. If something did not match, I had to resolve it before the order moved on.

That is the same basic discipline I would bring to financial records or reconciliations: check the evidence, identify the exception and fix or escalate it.

## How do you approach a new task? | Understand · Break-down · Check

**Remember:** understand · break-down · check

First I want to understand what the task is actually trying to achieve and what the finished output should look like.

Then I break it into smaller steps, identify the information or tools I need and work through it systematically.

Before I treat it as finished, I check the result against the original purpose and source information. If something is uncertain, I would rather ask or flag it than quietly make an assumption.

## How do you manage several tasks? | Prioritise · Track · Communicate

**Remember:** prioritise · track · communicate

I would first separate tasks by deadline, importance and dependency — especially anything another person is waiting on.

I like keeping a clear list of what is outstanding and what the next action is, rather than relying on memory.

If priorities conflict or I think a deadline is at risk, I would communicate early with the person responsible rather than wait until the deadline has passed.

## What would you do if you made an error? | Own · Correct · Prevent

**Remember:** own · correct · prevent

I would raise it rather than hide it, understand the impact, correct it through the proper process and make sure the relevant person knew.

Then I would look at why it happened. If there was a check, template or process change that could prevent the same error happening again, I would suggest it.

Accuracy matters, but so does how professionally you respond when something goes wrong.

## What do KYC and CDD mean to you? | Identity · Risk · Evidence

**Remember:** identity · risk · evidence

KYC is Know Your Customer and CDD is Customer Due Diligence.

My basic understanding is that the firm has to establish who the client and relevant beneficial owners are, understand the ownership and risk involved, obtain the required evidence and keep the records current.

I would not pretend to know Pivotal’s internal procedures yet, but I understand why the work has to be complete, accurate and properly documented.

## Why does documentation matter? | Evidence · Traceability · Compliance

**Remember:** evidence · traceability · compliance

In this kind of work, it is not enough for a number or action to be correct — there has to be evidence showing where it came from, who approved it and what happened.

Good supporting documentation makes work reviewable and traceable and helps demonstrate compliance.

That connects strongly with how I approach data analysis: I want to know the source, the transformation and why I can rely on the final result.

## Tell us about a data problem you solved | Clean · Reconcile · Explain

**Remember:** clean · reconcile · explain

One of my projects used public aircraft data from several sources. The records were incomplete and sometimes inconsistent, so the important part was not the visualisation at the end; it was cleaning the data and deciding what could actually be supported by the evidence.

I compared records, reconciled differences and deliberately left some cases unresolved where I could not justify a conclusion.

That taught me an important lesson for financial work as well: a clean-looking answer is not valuable if the underlying evidence is weak.

## What does process improvement mean to you? | Purpose · Control · Efficiency

**Remember:** purpose · control · efficiency

Process improvement is not just making something faster. First I would understand why the process exists and what control it provides.

Then I would look for repeated manual steps, unnecessary duplication or places where errors commonly occur.

If a change saves time while keeping or improving the control, that is useful. My data and programming background could help with that, but I would learn the existing process properly before trying to automate it.

## How would you research a financial or market topic? | Question · Sources · Summary

**Remember:** question · sources · summary

I would start by defining exactly what question needs to be answered, because otherwise research can become too broad.

Then I would use reliable primary or professional sources where possible, compare information rather than rely on one source and keep track of where the facts came from.

Finally I would summarise the answer for the person who needs it: what matters, what the evidence says and what remains uncertain.

If I had a useful observation, question or improvement idea, I would contribute it clearly and briefly.

As a co-op student I would not assume I knew more than the experienced team, but I also would not sit silently if I could add something useful or spot a problem.

## Final Recall | Accurate · Professional · Learn
