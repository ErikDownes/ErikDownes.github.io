## Company | What does Pivotal Corporate do?

Pivotal Corporate is an independently owned **Corporate Service Provider (CSP)** with more than 100 professionals across Shannon, Dublin, London and New York.

Its work sits behind companies, transactions and investment structures. Clients may need an entity formed, accounts maintained, board and company-secretarial work organised, cash movements administered, regulatory requirements met, or an international operation supported.

Its services include company formation and management, financial accounting and reporting, company-secretarial and governance services, transaction and cash management, compliance, tax and regulatory support, international expansion and restructuring, managing-agent services and staff augmentation.

The company works across client industries and asset classes including aviation, private equity, real estate, private debt, structured debt, infrastructure, technology and pharmaceuticals.

**Important distinction:** Pivotal can work on structures involving aircraft without being an aircraft-leasing company. The professional function is corporate services and financial operations; aviation is one client industry.

## Business model | What are clients paying for?

A client is paying for **reliable execution**: accurate books and records, deadlines met, documents complete, transactions progressed, queries answered and issues identified early.

That helps explain the company's emphasis on **Experience, Responsiveness and Delivery**.

**Experience** — understand the transaction, rules and client context.  
**Responsiveness** — deal with questions and problems quickly and clearly.  
**Delivery** — complete the work accurately and on time.

## Shannon | Why does the location stand out?

The Shannon office shows that international corporate and financial-services work can be delivered from the wider Limerick-Shannon business ecosystem while serving global clients and transactions.

## Role | What would I actually be doing?

The Co-op Student / Corporate Administrator role combines finance, accounting, administration and compliance support for international clients.

The specification includes journals and reconciliations, accounts payable and receivable, maintaining financial records, VAT and tax or regulatory reporting, company administration, legal and regulatory documentation, KYC/CDD support, research and process improvement.

The common thread is dependable information: transactions must be recorded correctly, supporting documents must be complete, deadlines matter and work must stand up to review.

## Role fit | Where does my evidence connect?

**Quantitative and financial foundation** → Financial Mathematics, Finance, Financial Accounting and Accounting for Financial Decision Making.

**Accuracy and data** → aircraft-data reconciliation and other projects where uncertain records are checked rather than forced.

**Communication** → the PCP comparison, where total costs and a graph turned several finance structures into a clear decision.

**Commercial administration** → work with orders, invoices, dispatch and information moving accurately between stages.

**Customer service and teamwork** → retail experience and university project work.

**Learning systems quickly** → experience moving between Excel, Python, R, MATLAB, Java, Git/GitHub and web tools.

## Questions | What could I ask Pivotal?
